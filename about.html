About — Ash Ferris

I’m interested in how minds work—and what happens when we try to build them.

I’m an applied AI engineer and data scientist with more than six years of experience designing and delivering production AI and data systems.

Before moving into applied machine learning, I researched cognition, creativity, and psychological measurement. That background still shapes the questions I ask: not only whether a system works, but how it reasons, where it fails, and how people learn to trust it.

My practice moves between research, engineering, and strategy. I enjoy translating ambiguous human work into reliable software, making complex systems legible, and finding the questions hidden beneath an apparently technical problem.

Outside the screen, I paint, draw, and pay close attention to design. These are not separate interests so much as other ways of observing, composing, and making sense of complexity.
