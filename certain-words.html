Experiment 02 — Model uncertainty

Certain words,
uncertain answers.

Can a model be certain about what to say next without knowing whether what it says is true?

Generation certainty

Token probability asks: given everything generated so far, how expected was this particular continuation?

Reported confidence

A confidence score asks the model to estimate whether its completed answer is correct. It is another generated response—not a direct reading of an internal belief.

When each signal helps

Token probability

  • Finds unstable words, names, classifications, and extracted values.
  • Shows when several continuations were locally plausible.
  • Supports ranking in tightly constrained prediction tasks.
  • Does not establish whether the completed claim is true.

Reported confidence

  • Can reflect ambiguity, missing evidence, or reasoning difficulty.
  • Can help decide when verification or escalation is warranted.
  • Needs calibration against observed correctness.
  • May repeat the same misconception as the original answer.
